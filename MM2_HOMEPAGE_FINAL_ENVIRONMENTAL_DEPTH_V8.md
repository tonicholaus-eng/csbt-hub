# MM2 Homepage — Final Environmental Depth & Cohesion Pass (V8)

Base used: **CSBT_MM2_HOMEPAGE_SPATIAL_DEPTH_V7**

This pass preserves the approved homepage structure and pushes only environmental depth, material separation, and visual cohesion.

## Summary
This is **not** a redesign. No sections, routes, or content blocks were added. The Weapon Vault artwork and page architecture remain intact.

## What changed

### 1) Dark architectural structure
- introduced subtler recessed wall divisions and structural framing in the background
- strengthened low-contrast environmental planes instead of relying on obvious graphic patterning
- added more believable room depth using darker charcoal layers and distant seams

### 2) Hero background refinement
- reduced the feeling of empty black space around the CSBT HUB hero
- added very dark environmental framing behind the hero area
- introduced subtler reflected red light and distant depth without reducing readability

### 3) Hero ↔ Weapon Vault continuity
- improved cross-page continuity using shared structural planes and restrained reflected light
- avoided adding a new bright red gradient bridge
- made the two halves feel more like one command room

### 4) Weapon Vault integration
- preserved the current Weapon Vault artwork size and composition
- refined the vault container surroundings so it reads more like an installed chamber
- improved edge continuation, wall depth, floor/base treatment, and surrounding structure
- reduced the feeling of “art inside a web card”

### 5) Feature card row balancing
- preserved all four cards and their layout/content
- increased the visual presence of **Trade Calculator** and **CSBT Exchange** decorative artwork so they better match the weight of the other cards
- kept artwork subdued and readable rather than turning cards into mini hero sections

### 6) Tonal/material hierarchy
- clarified the depth relationship between:
  - environment/background
  - secondary structural surfaces
  - intro panel / feature cards / stats strip
  - focal elements and active UI
- avoided globally brightening the page

### 7) Lower-half grounding
- strengthened extremely subtle floor/base cues beneath the feature cards and statistics strip
- improved the sense that the lower content sits inside a physical room

### 8) Sidebar environmental integration
- kept the existing sidebar design
- added slightly stronger inner crimson reflection and shared-room ambient depth
- strengthened active item presence without turning the whole rail red

### 9) Intro module refinement
- preserved content and layout
- made the intro card feel less like a flat website rectangle through richer charcoal surfaces, internal depth, and subtler integrated edges

### 10) Statistics strip refinement
- preserved layout and data
- improved icon presence, surface separation, and subtle top-edge lighting
- kept it secondary to the hero and vault

## Files changed
- `src/components/mm2/MM2HQHome.module.css`
- `src/app/globals.css`

## Deliverable intent
The homepage should now feel more like a **dark premium MM2 command center** where the environmental depth is felt before it is consciously noticed.
